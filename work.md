## Independent projects

### [Snowseer](https://aturner22.github.io/snowseer/) · 2026
Submitted to [SoTA](https://ilikethefuture.com/)'s [Minimal-Shot Autonomy Commission](https://sotaletters.substack.com/p/sota-commission-i-minimal-shot-autonomy). Snowseer finds a road within a snow plough's camera feed when it is buried ([demo](https://aturner22.github.io/snowseer/), [code](https://github.com/aturner22/snowseer)).

### [WIRE](https://wire-delta.vercel.app/) · 2025
First place in the [OpenWeather Challenge](https://openweather.co.uk/blog/post/openweather-challenge-award-ceremony-at-imperial-college-london). WIRE turns weather forecasts into hazard scores for vulnerable populations ([app](https://wire-delta.vercel.app/), [code](https://github.com/aturner22/WIRE)).

### [Elicitor](https://github.com/aturner22/thomas-orb) · 2025
Grand prize at [SoTA](https://ilikethefuture.com/)'s [Human Augmentation Hackathon](https://sotaletters.substack.com/p/results-from-sotas-recent-hackathons). Elicitor turns a conversation with a non-technical expert into a valid Bayesian prior ([code](https://github.com/aturner22/thomas-orb)).

### [Albed.io](https://github.com/aturner22/hackathon_lam) · 2025
Technical excellence prize at [SoTA](https://ilikethefuture.com/)'s [Geoengineering and Weather Hackathon](https://sotaletters.substack.com/p/weather-control-and-geoengineering). Albed.io is a geoengineering simulator modelling how an intervention in the atmosphere may influence nearterm weather patterns ([code](https://github.com/aturner22/hackathon_lam)).
