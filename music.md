I play saxophone and write music, with experience running from small jazz and pop groups to big bands. I'm part of [All You Can Eat](https://allyoucaneat.band/), [This Moment](https://www.instagram.com/this.moment.band/), the [Imperial College Big Band](https://www.instagram.com/icbigband/) and others across London. In the past, I directed the [Oxford University Jazz Orchestra](https://www.instagram.com/oxfordjazzorchestra/).

Recordings can be found on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@ashleyturner7986) and [Instagram](https://instagram.com/ashleyturnerr). I have also taught saxophone, improvisation and theory. I am always glad to hear about performance, session work and collaboration opportunities, please do get in touch.
