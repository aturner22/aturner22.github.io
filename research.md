I am based at [Imperial College London](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/) as part of the [Mathematics for our Future Climate](https://www.mfccdt.ac.uk/) Centre for Doctoral Training, supervised by [Nicola Gnecco](https://www.ngnecco.com/) and [Almut Veraart](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/people/a.veraart). My research is in statistics and machine learning with a focus on high-dimensional and heavy-tailed data. I examine representation learning and generative models that preserve the tails of data distributions, the theory behind them and their use in weather and climate science. I am also interested in forecasting, calibration, proper scoring rules and simulation-based inference. 

Previous studies include an MSc in Statistics at [Imperial College London](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/) and a BA in Economics and Management at the [University of Oxford](https://www.ox.ac.uk/).
